AR Chemistry Lab — User Guide
How to use this lab
What You Need
🖼️
6 Printed Image Targets Flat printed images — keep them unfolded on a surface
📱
Your Device Hold it steady — shaky hands cause images to lose tracking
💡
Good Lighting Avoid shadows falling directly on the images
Download & Print Image Targets (one per prop)
Main Station Heat / Lighter Ceramic Pot Sand Box Mixture Magnesium Stick
How Image Targets Work
👁️
Point Camera at an Image A 3D object appears on top of it — that's your virtual lab equipment
🔗
Bring Images Close Together Placing two images near each other triggers interactions (e.g. pot snaps to station)
📋
Follow the Instruction Panel The card at the bottom always tells you the next step
Experiment Flow
1
Show the Main Station imageThis anchors the experiment
2
Add the Ceramic PotBring its image close to the station
3
Add Thermite Mixture, then Magnesium RibbonOne at a time
4–5
Hold the Lighter for 5 secondsWatch the reaction! A quiz follows.
Tips for Best Results
📐
Keep images flat on a surfaceBent or tilted prints lose tracking quickly
📏
Hold device 30–50 cm above imagesToo close or too far reduces detection accuracy
🔄
Image lost? Re-scan itMove the device away then slowly back toward the image
💬
Stuck? Tap "Need a hint?"It appears automatically after 30 seconds of no progress
⚠️ Safety first: real thermite burns at ~2,500 °C and cannot be extinguished once lit. Never attempt this reaction outside a licensed industrial setting — that's exactly why you're doing it in AR.
